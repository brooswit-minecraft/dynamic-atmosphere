### Added
- Every atmospheric material (Vapor, Smoke, Dust, Void Gas, Exhaust, Slime, Obsidian Powder, Zombie Fog, Nether Gas, Glow Gas, Nether Fog) now blends its rendered colour toward a configurable sun colour (`sunTintColor`, default `0xFFCB82`) the more directly the camera looks at a visible sun, and toward a configurable moon colour (`moonTintColor`, default `0xA9C4E8`) the more directly it looks at a visible moon, fading smoothly through oblique angles and to no effect at all looking away. The blend is a bounded convex combination capped by `celestialTintMaxStrength` (default `0.35`, range 0-1) so a material never loses its own colour identity entirely. A body contributes nothing when it is below the horizon, in a dimension with no rendered sky bodies (End, Nether), or when the camera's own sky column is covered (`Level#canSeeSky`) or faded by rain, matching vanilla's own sun/moon visibility rules rather than assuming time of day alone means visible.
