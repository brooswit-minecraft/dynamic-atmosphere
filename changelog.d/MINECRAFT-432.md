### Changed
- Internal client rendering optimization: the volume renderer now snapshots client config once per frame instead of re-reading it per material/cell/slice, computes the sun/moon tint weight once per frame and each visible material's final RGB once per frame (instead of recomputing both per slice), shares one normalized camera basis across all of a frame's geometry generation instead of rebuilding it per volume, precomputes the 32 fixed reach-clipping plane normals once instead of per clipped cell, and folds the Vapor fog-darkness/distance-fade alpha adjustment into slice generation instead of copying the slice list a second time afterward. Tint computation (including its per-frame ray casts) is skipped outright when `celestialTintMaxStrength` is configured to `0`, without corrupting the smoothed sun/moon visibility state used when the tint is re-enabled later. No rendered output changes: colour, alpha, polygon vertices, slice ordering (including across mixed materials and GPU batches), and per-frame config-reload behavior are all unchanged.
