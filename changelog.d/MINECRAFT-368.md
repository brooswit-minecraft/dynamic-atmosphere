### Added
- Phantom: while actually airborne and moving (not merely existing), a Phantom now emits Obsidian Powder on the exact same interval-ticks schedule and OBSIDIAN_POWDER call site already used by Endermen, Endermites, the Ender Dragon, Witches, and Shulkers. A grounded or stationary Phantom does not emit.

### Changed
- Phantom attacks now deal zero damage, regardless of equipment, enchantments, or other mods' modifiers to the Phantom's attack-damage attribute.
